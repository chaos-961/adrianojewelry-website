In the workshop

Sixteen pieces, turned in your hand.

A cross-section of what we make at the bench — claw and bezel settings, bead-set pavé, hand-linked chain and mother-of-pearl.

Rings

  • Solitaire Round brilliant in a four-claw head, bead-set pavé running down the shoulders.
  • Eternity band Twenty-two stones shared between grain prongs, open gallery all the way round.
  • Cushion halo Round centre stone framed by a cushion-shaped halo on a pavé shank.
  • Bezel solitaire A rub-over bezel with a rolled lip — the setting we recommend for hands that work.

Earrings

  • Pavé huggies Squared hoop with stones bead-set around the outer edge and a click closure.
  • Huggie drops A pavé huggie carrying a claw-set round brilliant on a jump ring.
  • Threaders Fine box chain that threads through the lobe, finished with a bezel-set stone.
  • Clover studs Mother-of-pearl quatrefoil inside a beaded gold rim, post and butterfly back.

Bracelets

  • Station bracelet Four bezel-set stones spliced into a cable chain, with a lobster clasp and adjuster.
  • Screw bangle Solid oval bangle, eight screw motifs milled into the outer face, hinged.
  • Clover chain A single mother-of-pearl clover hung from hand-linked cable chain.
  • Tennis bracelet Forty claw-set stones in articulated box links, box clasp with a safety.

Necklaces

  • Bezel solitaire A single rub-over stone that slides on the chain rather than hanging from a bail.
  • Graduated seven Seven claw-set stones stepping down in size from the centre, linked to sit flat.
  • Clover pendant The clover motif again, this time on a fine cable chain.
  • Pavé bar A vertical bar channelled with small stones, hung from a wire bail.
