The work

From sketch to setting.

Every commission runs the same five stages. It is the same sequence whether the piece is new or a hundred years old and coming back to life.

  1. 01 / Sketch

    Graphite before anything is cut.

    Proportion, stone size, how it sits against the finger. Nothing is committed until the drawing is right.

  2. 02 / Wax

    Carved at full scale.

    The design cut in wax at the size it will be worn. This is the last chance to change your mind.

  3. 03 / Cast

    The wax burns away.

    Molten gold takes its place. One piece, one mould, no second copy.

  4. 04 / Set

    Seated by hand, under magnification.

    Each stone placed and closed individually. Millimetres decide whether it catches light.

  5. 05 / Finish

    Filed, polished, inspected.

    Then it goes in a box with your name on it.

The maker

Twenty-three years at one bench.

I grew up in a family of jewelers. I learned to repair and to design in Europe, then brought that training to the United States, where I’ve spent twenty-three years building a practice on it. European precision, American expectations.

What we do

Three ways in.

Custom design

From a sketch, a photograph, or a description you cannot quite draw. We work the proportions out on paper first, then in wax, and you see it at every stage before anything is cast.

Bring a sketch, a photograph, or a description

Repair

Resizing, clasp and chain work, stone tightening, prong rebuilding and professional cleaning. Most of it is done on site at the bench rather than sent away, so the piece does not leave the building.

Bring the piece · quoted before any work begins

Restoration

Heirlooms brought back without losing what makes them old. Worn settings rebuilt, lost stones matched, original character kept rather than polished away.

Bring the piece, and bring the story
