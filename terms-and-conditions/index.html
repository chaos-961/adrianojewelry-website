Return and Refund Policy

Jewelry is bought for a reason, and sometimes the piece is not right. If that happens, here is exactly how we handle it.

Terms of return

You may return an eligible piece within fourteen (14) days of receiving it, or of collecting it from the shop. Items bought as gifts between 1 November and 24 December may be returned until 15 January. We cannot accept a return requested after the window has closed.

State of return

The piece must come back to us:

  • unworn and unaltered: not resized, engraved, polished, plated or reset by anyone else;
  • in its original condition, free of scratches, marks and signs of wear;
  • in its original box and packaging, with every tag still attached and unbroken;
  • with all paperwork: the receipt, and any laboratory certificate, appraisal or warranty card that came with it.

A missing certificate is expensive to replace, and its cost will be deducted from the refund. We inspect every returned piece, and where an item comes back worn or altered we may decline the return and send it back to you.

Reason for return

You do not need a reason to return an eligible stock piece within the window. Tell us anyway if you can; it helps us. Where an item arrives damaged, faulty or not what you ordered, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photographs of the piece and the packaging. We will repair it, replace it, or refund it in full including shipping, at your choice.

What we cannot take back

  • custom and bespoke commissions, and special orders made to your specification;
  • engraved, resized or otherwise personalised pieces;
  • earrings and other pierced jewelry, for hygiene reasons;
  • items sold as final sale, clearance, estate or as-is, which are marked as such at the time of purchase;
  • completed repair and restoration work, but if a repair is not right, bring it back and we will put it right;
  • gift cards.

None of this affects your rights where an item is genuinely faulty.

Process for return

  1. Contact us first. Call the shop on 857-991-1679 or come in, with your receipt or order number to hand. Do not ship anything back before you have spoken to us: unannounced parcels cannot be traced to an order, and we cannot accept liability for them.
  2. We confirm eligibility and give you a return reference.
  3. Bring it in, or ship it. Returning in person at 333 Washington Street is fastest. If you ship, use an insured, tracked, signature-required service, insure it for the full purchase price, and pack it so nothing moves inside the box. The piece remains your responsibility until it reaches us.
  4. We inspect the piece within three business days of receiving it and confirm the outcome.

Process of refund

  • Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. We cannot refund to a different card or account.
  • We process the refund within five business days of approving the return. Your bank or card issuer then takes a further five to ten business days to post it, which is outside our control.
  • We refund the price paid and any sales tax. Original shipping is refunded only where the item was faulty, damaged or wrongly sent.
  • Return shipping is at your cost unless the item was faulty, damaged or wrongly sent, in which case we cover it.
  • Deposits on custom work are refundable only to the extent that labour and materials have not yet been committed.
  • Gifts returned with a gift receipt are refunded as store credit to the person returning them; refunds to the original payment method go to the original purchaser.

Exchanges

We are happy to exchange an eligible piece within the same window, on the same conditions. Where the new piece costs more, the difference is payable at the time of exchange; where it costs less, we refund the difference to the original payment method. First resizing on a ring bought here is free within thirty days.
